# Code of conduct

VCode welcomes respectful, constructive participation. Treat people with dignity, discuss the work rather than the person and make space for different experience levels and perspectives.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate disruption and publication of private information are not accepted. Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to [podpora@zeleznalady.cz](mailto:podpora@zeleznalady.cz). Reports are handled as confidentially as practical.
