## Before you drive

1. Open **Vehicles** and add the car you are going to use.
2. Add anyone you want to track under **Passengers**.
3. Allow location access. Enable background location if you need reliable recording while the phone is locked.
4. Return to **Rides** and check that the correct vehicle is selected.

## Your first recording

Tap **Start ride** when you are ready to leave. Do not operate the app while driving; make changes before the journey or after stopping safely.

When you finish, review the route, vehicle and passengers. You can add a purpose and costs later.

## If recording is interrupted

Jízda saves recovery checkpoints during the journey. When reopened, it offers to resume or safely complete the unfinished record. If location data is missing, check the app’s permission in iPhone Settings.
