## Before you drive

This guide describes the upcoming expanded version of Jízda. Controls in the version currently available on the App Store may differ.

1. Open **Vehicles** and add the car you are going to use.
2. Add anyone you want to track under **Passengers**.
3. Allow location access if you want to record a route using GPS. Recording while the phone is locked requires the appropriate background permission. You can also enter a journey manually.
4. From **Rides**, open the GPS recording form and select the correct vehicle and passengers.

## Your first recording

Start GPS recording from the form when you are ready to leave. Do not operate the app while driving; make changes before the journey or after stopping safely.

When you finish, review the route, vehicle and passengers. You can add a purpose and costs later.

## If recording is interrupted

Jízda saves recovery checkpoints during the journey. When reopened, it offers to resume or safely complete the unfinished record. If location data is missing, check the app’s permission in iPhone Settings.
