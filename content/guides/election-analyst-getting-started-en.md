Open the [election atlas](https://stratos.zeleznalady.cz/ea/). The public app is in Czech and does not require registration.

## Find results for your area

1. Open **Výsledky voleb** (Election results) and choose an election type and date.
2. Search for a municipality or another available area. Where names match, check the region and other identifying information.
3. Explore turnout, published results and explanations of the individual measures.

Selections depend on available sources. A missing value is not a measured zero, and future election rounds have no results.

## Open the map or a comparison

In **Mapa voleb** (Election map), zoom to a place and select an available layer. Use **Porovnání** (Comparison) to explore another period. Read the limitations before interpreting a difference where geographical coverage or vote allocation has changed.

## Need more detail?

Switch to **Pro odborníky** (For specialists). This view works with the same public data; it is not a sign-in or a separate access permission. Always check a candidature’s election and area, not just its name.

## Check a source and keep your selection

Open **Zdroje a metodika** (Sources and methodology) for source information and explanations of calculations. Use a table’s CSV export where available. Share the link to keep the context of a prepared view.

The atlas is independent, not an official Czech Statistical Office service. A data-check finding does not itself establish electoral fraud or replace examination of the original sources. Loading results and maps requires an internet connection.
