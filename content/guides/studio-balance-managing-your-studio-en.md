Studio Balance brings together the schedule, bookings and everyday management of a movement studio. This guide walks you through five common tasks. The management screens shown use Czech, so the steps keep the Czech button names and explain them in English.

## Cancel a single class

1. Open **„Rozvrh“ → „Zrušit jeden termín“** — Schedule → Cancel a single session.
2. Choose the date and the specific class.
3. Enter a reason and check the details. Tick **„Chci zrušit pouze tento termín a jeho rezervace bez poplatku“** (Cancel only this session and its bookings without a fee).
4. Select **„Potvrdit zrušení tohoto termínu“** (Confirm cancellation of this session). The button becomes available after you tick the confirmation.

Other sessions stay unchanged. Booked clients receive a message in their account. No cancellation fee is charged when the studio cancels a class.

## Change a regular class’s capacity or price

1. In **„Rozvrh“** (Schedule), find the regular time slot and open **„Upravit pravidelnou lekci“** (Edit a regular class).
2. Choose **„Kapacitu“** (Capacity) or **„Cenu“** (Price), select the sessions and set **„Platí od“** (Effective from).
3. Enter the new value and an explanation. Select **„Zkontrolovat dopad“** (Review the impact), then **„Potvrdit změnu“** (Confirm the change).

For price changes, you explicitly choose whether to update already confirmed future bookings. Capacity cannot be reduced below the number of booked participants.

## See who is coming and confirm attendance

1. Open **„Přihlášení“** (Booked participants) for the specific session.
2. Check the list. After the class ends, mark the people who did not attend.
3. Record any substitute instructor and enter the instructor’s pay, then select **„Potvrdit účast ostatních a uložit lekci“** (Confirm attendance for everyone else and save the class).

Confirm attendance after the class. Any applicable fees for non-attendance are shown before you save.

## Get the monthly instructor report

1. Open **„Výkaz instruktorů“** (Instructor report) and choose the month.
2. Review the classes, teaching hours and instructor pay. Fill in any missing details that are flagged.
3. Print or download the report.

The report includes classes confirmed as taught. You enter instructor pay yourself; missing amounts remain flagged for completion.

## Publish news for clients

1. Open **„Novinky“** (News) and write a heading, a short summary and the full text.
2. Choose a publication date and tick **„Publikovat v klientské aplikaci“** (Publish in the client app).
3. Select **„Uložit novinku“** (Save the news item).

Without publication, the text remains a draft. Published news appears to signed-in clients no earlier than the time you set.
