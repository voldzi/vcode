## Logging that stays out of the way

Kalorické tabulky helps people record food quickly even without a stable connection. A verified catalogue reduces uncertainty when selecting an item.

## Recipes and real portions

Custom recipes can be divided into portions and reused. The diary better reflects what a person actually eats.

## Trends without judgement

Insights show change over time in a neutral, privacy-conscious way. The goal is useful orientation rather than pressure for perfection.
