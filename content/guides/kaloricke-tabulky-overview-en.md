## Start with one meal

Moje porce connects a food and water diary, personal recipes, pantry and shopping list. You do not need to use everything at once. [Open the app’s free core features](https://mojeporce.cz/domu), select a food, adjust the portion and confirm the entry.

**The app interface and linked resources currently use primarily Czech.** This English introduction does not imply an English app interface. The detailed guide is maintained alongside the app:

- [First entry and everyday tasks](https://mojeporce.cz/pruvodce)
- [Keeping a food diary](https://mojeporce.cz/pruvodce/kaloricky-denik)
- [Recipes, shopping lists and pantry](https://mojeporce.cz/pruvodce/recepty-nakupy-a-zasoby)
- [Food photos, labels and receipts](https://mojeporce.cz/pruvodce/fotografie-jidla-a-uctenky)

## From a recipe to the portion you ate

Choose your own recipe in My Kitchen, check your pantry and update your shopping list. After cooking, log the portion you actually ate. Stored loyalty cards can be kept handy; storing a card does not automatically connect it to a retailer.

## Phone installation, offline logging and AI

You can add the progressive web app to your phone’s home screen or install it from a supported browser. On iPhone, use Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen.

You can log entries in the local diary without a connection. AI requires internet access. Review and edit its suggestions before saving; a photo does not guarantee an accurate portion and does not replace weighing. Check sources and quantities when selecting food, as information may be incomplete.

## Premium and support

The basic manual diary, recipes, pantry and lists are free. Check the [current Premium page](https://mojeporce.cz/premium) for Premium beta features, access options and information about AI credits. Self-service subscription purchasing has not launched.

See [About the app](https://mojeporce.cz/o-aplikaci), [privacy information](https://mojeporce.cz/ochrana-soukromi) and [support](https://mojeporce.cz/podpora) for more information. These linked pages are primarily in Czech.
