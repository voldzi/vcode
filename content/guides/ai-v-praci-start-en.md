Open [AI v práci](https://games.zeleznalady.cz/prezentaceAI/). The practice app and its documents are in Czech. You can start without an account.

## Attach, instruct, check

1. Choose the first-case guide on the home screen or another available workplace story.
2. Open the prepared document and read its context. Your own files are not uploaded.
3. Choose an instruction and explore the prepared response. Pause the typing animation or reveal the complete answer.
4. Return to the source. Separate confirmed facts from proposals and check what is still missing.
5. Draft an instruction or working approach for your own approved Copilot.

## Optional saved work

To return to your work, use a nickname and private return code. Keep the code safe: anyone who knows it can open your work. Saving and retrieving personal work requires a connection.

## Home screen and offline practice

In supported browsers you can add the app to your home screen. Public examples work offline after an initial visit; attachments after you explicitly download them. Open the relevant story online before you travel.

## What to expect

All stories and documents are fictional, and responses are prepared in advance. The app does not use a live AI model or replace your workplace Copilot. It is independent practice, not a Microsoft service or a certified course.
