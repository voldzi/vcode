Start with the situation you need to handle. Record an ordinary massage payment directly against the appointment; you do not need to open the financial records after every client.

The steps below retain the Czech labels shown in the application, with their meaning explained in English.

## A client calls to book an appointment

1. Open **Kalendář** (Calendar) and choose **Objednat klienta** (Book a client).
2. Find an existing client, or choose **Nový klient** (New client) and enter their first name, surname and phone number or email address.
3. Select the massage, date and agreed time. Check the details before saving.
4. Select **Potvrdit rezervaci** (Confirm booking) and check that the appointment appears in the calendar.

## The appointment is complete and the client has paid

1. Open the client's booking in **Kalendář** (Calendar).
2. Confirm that the client attended. In the payment section, choose **Přidat platbu** (Add payment).
3. Check the amount, payment method and date. Save the payment and check its saved status.
4. Save any notes about the massage against the appointment.

The payment also appears in the financial records. Do not enter it there again as another income item.

## An invoice arrives or you make a purchase

1. Under **Více** (More), open **Doklady a výdaje** (Documents and expenses).
2. Select a photo or file, or drag the document into the marked area. On supported devices, you can also use **Vložit ze schránky** (Paste from clipboard).
3. Choose **Uložit doklad** (Save document). Open it and check the supplier, date, amount and document number against the original. If you use AI to read the details, check its result too.
4. Select one or more categories and choose **Potvrdit doklad** (Confirm document).
5. If you have already paid for the purchase, open **Finance a daňová evidence** (Finances and tax records) and choose **Zapsat jinou platbu** (Record another payment). Record the expense and link the uploaded document.

A saved or confirmed invoice is not yet a paid expense. Enter the payment date according to when you actually paid.

## A client needs a receipt or invoice

1. Under **Více** (More), open **Finance a daňová evidence** (Finances and tax records).
2. Choose **Vystavit fakturu či účtenku** (Issue an invoice or receipt) and select the appropriate document type.
3. Check the details and amount. Link a receipt to the payment you have already recorded; issuing an invoice does not itself record money received.
4. Download the completed document and print it if needed.

## You are preparing your year-end records

1. Select the correct year in the financial records.
2. Review documents awaiting a check, missing payments and the year review. Complete the asset overview and other items where relevant.
3. Under **Stáhnout podklady** (Download records), choose the report or document archive you need.
4. Close the year only after checking and completing the necessary details.

These records help you prepare a tax return; the application does not file it for you. If you are unsure how to classify an expense, leave it for review instead of guessing.

## You are unsure what a field means

Open the question mark next to its name. For help with a whole task, use **Pomoc s financemi** (Help with finances) or **Pomoc s nákupním dokladem** (Help with a purchase document). The guidance explains the next step; the optional AI assistant does not confirm or change your records for you.

## EET is in preparation

Payments are currently recorded in your own records. They are not automatically sent to the Czech Financial Administration. A direct connection to EET, the Czech electronic sales registration system, is being prepared; enabling it will be a separate step. Saving a payment or changing tax settings does not enable it now.
