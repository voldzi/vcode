## Show information

Open **Layers** and choose the areas you need. The number beside a layer is the amount of currently available objects; zero may simply mean there is no data in the current map view.

Tap a symbol to open its summary. Expand the object panel for actions, source details and data age.

## Your location

Tap the location control once to centre the map. Tapping it again changes heading follow mode. Drag the map to stop automatic following.

## Slow or missing map tiles

COP uses saved tiles and a network cache. On a weak connection it may show the last available map. If an area is blank, keep the app open briefly or change the zoom level.
