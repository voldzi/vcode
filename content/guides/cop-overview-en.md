## A shared picture

COP combines public and operational information on one map. Layers can be selected for the task so important details are not lost in noise.

## From awareness to decision

Users can check traffic, alerts, restrictions or infrastructure and plan a route for the way they intend to travel.

## Public and professional

The public experience supports everyday users. Extended workflows add reporting, communication and coordination according to assigned permissions.
