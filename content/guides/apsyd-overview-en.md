## One controlled process

APSYD gives reception, psychologists, laboratory staff and management a shared environment for preparing and delivering psychodiagnostic assessments. Each role sees the tasks and information it needs.

## From arrival to result

The site prepares the participant, assessment purpose and approved battery. Testing runs in a controlled environment and a psychologist reviews the result before professional use.

## Trust and traceability

Sensitive data, permissions and important actions are covered by the security and audit design. APSYD Central coordinates multiple sites without sharing their databases.
