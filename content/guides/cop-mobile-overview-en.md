## The situation in your pocket

COP Mobile brings the situation map, alerts and important layers to iPhone. Its interface supports quick orientation in the field.

## Reports in the right place

Users can submit a report with a location and media. Under the configured rules, it becomes part of the shared COP picture.

## Communication in context

Secure chat and calls remain connected to the situational environment, reducing the need to switch between unrelated tools.
