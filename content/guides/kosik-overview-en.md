## Start with verified information

[Živý Košík](https://games.zeleznalady.cz/kosik/) is a website and app being prepared for Košík, Doubravany, Tuchom and Sovolusky. The pilot is for reviewing the design and features. It does not yet replace the municipality's official website or electronic noticeboard. Check binding notices, current dates, municipal charges and instructions on the [existing Košík municipal website](http://www.kosik-obec.cz/) or with the municipal office.

The app interface is in Czech. The new leaf motif is a proposed Živý Košík brand, rather than the municipality's official coat of arms.

## Choose a locality

The public overview lets you follow the entire municipality or choose Košík, Doubravany, Tuchom or Sovolusky. News and events can apply to one or more localities. Municipality-wide announcements apply to all four.

News contains information published by authorised administrators. The calendar supports community events, council meetings and waste collections. Hazardous waste, bulky waste and asbestos have separate labels. The app does not determine acceptance rules or safe asbestos-handling procedures: follow verified municipal notices and instructions from an authorised collection service. An empty pilot calendar means no verified date has been published yet.

The community section is ready for fire groups and associations across the municipality. Information about the fire groups in Košík, Doubravany and Tuchom, and other associations, will be added after the municipality verifies their names and contacts. Material about the new land-use plan is a draft for administrators; binding consultation dates are never invented or estimated.

## Add the app to your home screen

You can use the website in a normal browser without installing anything. If your browser offers installation, add Živý Košík to your desktop or home screen. On an iPhone this option is usually in Safari's share menu. Availability and wording depend on the device and browser.

The PWA can retain public content for viewing without a connection. Reconnect and check the current notice before making an important decision. Reports and votes cannot be successfully submitted offline. Administration and private reports do not belong in the public offline content.

## Notifications are your choice

Device notifications are optional. Enable them when you want them, then accept the browser's permission request. Some devices first require adding the PWA to the home screen. Where supported, choose your topics and localities. Municipality-wide information remains shared.

You can turn notifications off in the app or in your browser or device settings. Delivery also depends on connectivity and the device's operating system. Website notifications do not replace emergency services or guaranteed emergency alerts.

Email subscriptions are implemented, but an email-sending service has not yet been configured for the pilot. Until then email subscriptions remain inactive and the app does not claim to have sent a message. Once the municipality configures delivery, subscribing will require confirming a link received by email. Subscribers will then be able to change topics and localities or unsubscribe.

## Submit a report and keep its tracking link

Give your report a clear title, description and location. You can add a photo and an optional contact address. Avoid including information the municipality does not need to resolve the issue. Reports are not published as news; authorised administrators see them in the private administration area.

After acceptance, keep the tracking link. It contains an access key and allows the status and replies to be viewed. Anyone who obtains that link can view the same information, so do not share it publicly. Attachments and administrators' internal notes are not included in public tracking. Treat a report as submitted only after the app confirms acceptance.

## Polls indicate opinions

Polls are informal surveys. The app limits repeat voting in the same browser but does not verify a voter's identity or residence. Results are not an official vote, referendum or verified account of every resident's opinion.

## The municipality manages its own content

Authorised staff use administration to prepare news, pages, events, documents and polls. Drafts are not public. Staff can check the text, localities and selected publishing channel before publication. Roles distinguish editors, publishers and administrators; publishing actions require TOTP two-factor verification. Recovery codes shown during setup should be stored safely by the account holder.

Reports have a separate private overview with status, internal notes and replies. Grant opportunities are a private working record of providers, deadlines, amounts and preparation status. They are not an automatic promise of funding. The municipality publishes the draft land-use-plan material only after its content has been verified.

The delivery overview distinguishes queued messages, delivery attempts and errors. Acceptance by a provider does not confirm that a resident has read the message. Failed actions need to be handled according to their displayed status.

## Help and current municipal information

Use the municipal contact details in the app and verify them on the existing website. Address questions about your data or report to the municipal office. This guide describes pilot features; it is not a legal audit or a security certification.
