## One shared source of truth

STRATOS connects strategy with projects, finance, contracts, risk and decisions. Every output can show the basis behind it.

## From need to outcome

The organisation sees the relationship between a need, its plan, delivery and actual impact. Changes are not hidden in isolated spreadsheets.

## Knowledge under control

Governed knowledge and an audit trail preserve the meaning of decisions through changes in people and organisational structure.
