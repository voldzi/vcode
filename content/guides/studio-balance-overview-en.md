## Find the right class

Studio Balance presents classes, their focus and the current schedule in one calm experience. Clients can quickly see what suits them.

## Reserve without calling

Booking shows available capacity and leads directly to confirming a place. Schedule changes remain visible in the same place.

## Clarity for clients and the studio

The client account simplifies booking management. The studio sees capacity and attendance without separate lists.
