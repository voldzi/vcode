Visits, practice activity and links

In AI v práci, we count openings of the public practice app and aggregate activity milestones: starting or resuming an exercise, the first newly completed playback of a prepared answer, the first opening of a cited passage, newly completing all main answers for the selected mode, and an explicitly confirmed restart. Optional bonuses are not required for completion. We do not assess the correctness of solutions or individual performance. Previously completed answers or exercises are not counted retrospectively when restored.

Each milestone has a fixed category. The app keeps only temporary flags in the memory of the open attempt to avoid sending milestones repeatedly; it creates no persistent analytics identifier for the attempt or participant. A reload or a repeated exercise may be counted again. Aggregate counts therefore do not represent unique people or an exact individual journey through an exercise. We also continue to count four fixed categories of footer links: Moje porce, Městem hrou, Myslopis and VCode. A click does not establish use of the destination app. The private dashboard shows separate collection start dates for the different types of measurement.

We send only a fixed category and a constant label for the practice app. We do not send original or destination link URLs, referral sources, URL parameters or fragments, page titles, link text, nicknames, return codes, participant, group or story identifiers, chat, notes, answers or any other work content. Administration, the instructor interface and APIs are excluded from this measurement. Analytics is not linked to participant accounts or saved exercises. It uses no analytics cookies, local storage, login identity or screen recording.

Collection is handled by the shared VCode/Umami service. On delivery, the server processes technical connection data, particularly the IP address and browser and device type. The IP address is not stored in readable form in the analytics database. A derived identifier changes daily and does not identify a participant account; we do not retain derived geographic location. Operational and security logs are separate from analytics.

With Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, or while offline, nothing is sent or stored for later delivery. The app and its links work with measurement disabled and do not wait for delivery. Only authorised people can view statistics after signing in. Analytics records, including operational backups, are retained for at most 180 days. Operator: VCode; contact: podpora@zeleznalady.cz.
