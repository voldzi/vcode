## Claims and editorial limits

- Actual calendar, client cards, notes, document upload, payment records, financial overview and contextual guidance are described in both languages.
- The English copy intentionally preserves the distinction between a recorded payment and an uploaded purchase document, between ordinary guidance and optional AI, and between active features and preparation.
- AI-assisted reading requires the operator’s review of the original. No autonomous saving, tax assessment or legal guarantee is offered.
- Finance is income/expense and year-end supporting recordkeeping, primarily for Czech non-VAT businesses claiming actual expenses. It is not advertised as full accounting, tax filing or a bank integration.
- EET is explicitly **in preparation**. Ordinary payments are not currently sent to the financial administration. No activation date, certification or included operational service is promised.
- Additional practices receive an individually agreed solution; no public price, automatic theme selection or immediate self-service availability is claimed.
- Existing client-facing help remains separate; the new operator guide supplements it.
